Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about how ATOM works? We've got answers.

Do customers need to download an app?

No downloads required! Customers simply open their smartphone camera, scan the table QR code, and the menu loads instantly in their mobile browser.

How do orders reach the kitchen?

When a customer checks out, the platform automatically formats the order details and streams them straight into your kitchen counter via WhatsApp.

Can I update menu items or prices?

Yes, absolutely. You can log into your mobile owner dashboard from any smartphone to toggle item stock status or edit prices instantly in real-time.

Is there any commission fee on food orders?

Zero commissions! You keep 100% of your earnings. We only charge a predictable, low monthly subscription fee.
